## What was done

### The schedule (r261027-6iom)

**DAY makes it** (`js/cal.js` "The schedule", `CAL.newSchedule`):
- An empty day's first button is **make a schedule** (it was "schedule from do"). A day's head has **schedule** (its own, to change) or **new schedule**. PLAN's sent list button is **schedule** (it was "export").
- A column of items, each with a switch, its name and its calendar, a start and a duration (− / +, 15 min a step).
- His five, from `plan.schedule` (new in Config): morning routine 2h, workout 1h, the day's **blocks** 1h each, evening routine 1h30, activity 1h.
- An item starts where the one before it ends. A start set by hand pins that one, and the items after it run on from it. The day's start ("from 07:00") moves the rest. ‹ › change the day.
- Switched off, an item keeps its place and makes no event.
- **Edit mode**: ↑ ↓ (the blocks move as one group, and past each other), "+ an item of your own" (renamed in place), ✕, and each item's calendar.
- **What he had on is remembered** (`cal_sched_v1`): which items, their names, durations, calendars and order. The blocks never are: they are the day's.

**The blocks are PLAN's** (`PLAN.blocksFor`):
- the tasks PLAN sent with a block label, due that day, in the order they were queued;
- or the rows picked on the sent list, whatever their label;
- today's blocks on DO too.

Each one's calendar is its project's (`plan.calendars`), else its DO label's project's, else `plan.schedule.blockCal` (01A5 | no work).

**Saving sends it to Google Calendar** (`PLAN.exportSchedule`):
- It writes the day's Todoist `@import` task, "schedule <day>", `template: schedule`, `mode: schedule`. Each item that is on is one line, down the day: `HH:MM · minutes · calendar · name`. The time is resolved and the calendar is its own name, so the 22:00 agent only transcribes. That's the "better architecture" `SCHEDULE-AGENT-PROMPT.md` §3.1 asked for.
- Saving the same day again rewrites that task (`plan_sched_tasks_v1`). Two tasks for one day would be a day the agent refuses.
- Only once the task has landed is the day kept in DAY (`mode: schedule`, its items in the record's `sched`, so opening it again brings them back). That's §9's rule: DAY never draws a day it didn't send. A failure says "not sent · …" and keeps the editor.
- CAL still makes no network call of its own; PLAN does the one write.

**The agent learns `mode: schedule`** (`SCHEDULE-AGENT-PROMPT.md` §1): parse the lines, archive the day the `full` way (copy to `00B | schedule 2`, then delete), write each line. **The routine holds its own copy of the prompt**: until it is replaced, the agent will refuse a `mode: schedule` task as malformed, notify, and leave the task.

**Kept for now, unreachable from the new buttons:** the slot export panel (`PLAN.openExport`: templates, b1a…b3b, `blocks` / `full` / `patch`) and DAY's "+ do" for a day that has slots. They stay until he has used the new schedule, then they go.

**PLAN's task form is unchanged.** The time estimate is already off by default, and the block labels (b1, b2, b3) stay, because DO's tiles and his homebase filters read them. "plan should just be plan what you're going to do, no time attatched to it but" is cut off after "but"; I read it as: PLAN says what, the schedule says when.

## What was done

### CURATE ticks off — task 4

`js/create.js`, `css/create.css`, `js/config.js`, `index.html`, `ROOT.md`.

Through 4.1 this tab was **read only**, and the reasoning was written down in
three separate files: closing a curate task is DO's job, filing one is PLAN's,
and a third app with an opinion about the same list is how two of them end up
disagreeing. That argument does not cover this request, and saying so is the
whole decision here. A record you have gone and found is *done*; walking to
another tab to say so is the errand that ends with a list nobody trusts.

So the invariant **narrowed** rather than went away:

- a curate row can be **closed and reopened**, and that is the entire set of
  writes CREATE makes anywhere;
- it still never moves, reschedules, renames or creates a task.

**The row is now two targets, not one.** It was a single `<a>` wrapping
everything. A button inside an anchor is not markup browsers have an agreed
answer for, so the tick box and the link out are siblings inside a flex row: the
box on the left closes it, the body opens it in Todoist. Visually it is still one
card — the split is in the hit areas.

**The write is optimistic, and reversible exactly.** `Todoist.call('/tasks/<id>/close')`
— the same call DO and TEND already make, and the safest one in the API — with
`/reopen` as its inverse. The row is struck through the moment it is tapped,
because a list this long stops feeling answerable if every tap waits on a
network. If the call does not land, the row goes back to **the state that was
captured before the change**, never to "the opposite of what it is now": a parent
carries its subtasks, and assuming the inverse would quietly reopen a subtask
nobody touched. That is now a §6 note.

**A ticked row stays where it is.** Struck through and dimmed rather than
removed — a row that vanished would take the only evidence of a mis-tap with it.
It stops being *counted* immediately, in its group's number and in the band.

**A parent carries its subtasks, both ways**, because Todoist does: closing a
task closes what is under it and reopening brings them back. Mirroring that
locally keeps the screen and the account saying the same thing until the next
refetch drops the row altogether. One call, three rows.

`closed` is a new field on the cached row and is read through `normalise()` like
every other, so a tick survives a reload and does not flicker back to open before
the refetch. Two guards: no key saved means no call and no tick, and a row whose
call has not answered cannot be tapped into a second, contradicting one.

### Labels in their own colours — task 5

`js/create.js`, `css/create.css`, `js/config.js`, `index.html`.

`create.curate.labelColors` (default on) draws each task's labels in the colour
Todoist gives them, read from `root_labels_v1` — the shared label cache DO fills
and PLAN refreshes — so a label is the same colour in every app that draws one.
The colours are asked for beside the tasks in the same `Promise.all`, so a first
draw already has them; `Todoist.labels()` answers from cache when it is fresh and
never rejects. **A colour that is not cached is not invented** — that label is
drawn plain.

The chip is the quietest box that still reads as one: the label's own hue at 14%
behind it, no border, `--lb-c` written on the element the way `--st-c` and
`--pr-c` already are. The due date keeps its muted grey and is not a chip.

The switch is in settings → create. It lives in the panel's **static markup**, so
unlike the content-editor switches it is not redrawn by `renderContent()` —
`Config.subscribe` → `CREATE.renderSettings()` is what puts the `on` class back.

### Three shapes — tasks 1, 2, 3

`css/create.css`.

**The session hours (task 1).** `.cr-ses` is `[what you did] [hours] [×]` in a
`space-between` row, so the hours ended wherever the sentence beside them stopped
and the × sat outboard of them. Now: the text takes the slack (`flex:1 1 auto`),
the hours are pushed to the right edge (`margin-left:auto`), right-aligned in a
box with a floor width, and set in tabular figures — so `1h30` over `45m` over
`12h` line up on the same edge instead of each starting somewhere different.

**The gap under the sticky title (task 2).** `.ns-create .cnt` was horizontal
padding only, so a sub-screen's first block started flush against its own sticky
header — which is exactly where a work's area tag and name are. `.hd + .cnt` now
takes top padding: the same padding DO, LEARN, LOG, PLAN and STORE already have
on all four sides, asked for only where there is a header. The shelf is untouched
by construction — it has no `.hd`, it has the wordmark block.

**Rounded squares, not pills (task 3).** The sort chips, the meta chips, the
stage stepper and the session-kind chips take `--r3` instead of `--r-pill`. The
dots, the rails and the progress bar keep `--r-pill`, because a circle is not a
button — and a harness check now says so, so "no more pills" cannot be read as
"delete every `--r-pill`".

### The interface has a voice — task 6

`js/prefs.js`, `js/shell.js`, `js/settings.js`, `ROOT.md`.

Three notes — `tap` (a control under a finger), `nav` (a slide arriving), `ok` (a
message) — **synthesised**: a sine or triangle through a gain envelope, none
longer than a twentieth of a second. Nothing is downloaded, so there is no asset
to fetch, no cache to think about and no licence to keep for a click. The
envelope is ramped rather than switched, because a gain that steps clicks, and a
click is the one thing a sound this quiet cannot afford.

**Wired in three places, all in `shell.js`, and nowhere else:** one capture-phase
`pointerdown` listener on the document that fires for anything that looks
pressable, `Shell.go` when the slide actually changes, and `Shell.toast`. No app
carries a line of it, so none of the eleven modules can end up with its own idea
of what a button sounds like — and if a press ought to sound, the fix is to make
it a control. `pointerdown` rather than `click` because the sound is feedback for
the *press*: a click fires after the finger lifts, which is late enough to sound
like a second event.

Two rules keep it out of the way.

- **Nothing exists until it is asked for.** No `AudioContext` is constructed
  while the setting is off, so an install that never turns sound on never builds
  an audio graph at all. The first one is built inside the gesture that plays the
  first note, which is the only moment a browser will allow it — one made at boot
  is born `suspended` and plays silence with no error anywhere. That is a §6 note
  now.
- **One sound per gesture.** A tab press is a pointerdown *and* a nav, and
  hearing both is the difference between "smooth" and "cheap", so a play within
  55 ms of the last one is dropped rather than layered. That also covers a
  dragged slider and a held key.

Two prefs: `sounds` (bool, **off** by default) and `soundLevel` (0.05–1, default
0.3), both in behaviour beside the haptic and both in the behaviour reset. Off by
default for the reason haptics is: it is the one setting that can embarrass
someone in a quiet room.

## Verification

`node test/harness.mjs` — **930 checks, all green** (896 at 4.1.2). 34 new, 2
rewritten.

The two rewritten ones are the read-only invariant and the note on screen that
stated it. The replacement is deliberately tighter than the thing it replaces:
there must be **exactly one** `Todoist.call(` in `create.js`, its path must be
`/close` or `/reopen`, its method must be the only method literal in the file,
and no `PUT`/`PATCH`/`DELETE` may appear. A second write is now a decision
someone has to make on purpose rather than an edit that slips through.

The tick is driven through the real DOM against a scripted Todoist: the call that
actually goes out, the row and its two subtasks going down on one call, the group
count and the band count dropping, the record on disk carrying `closed`, the
reopen putting all three back, a refused call restoring the row *and* the record,
and a tap with no key saved sending nothing and ticking nothing.

The sound is driven against a stubbed `AudioContext`: nothing constructed while
the setting is off, one context built inside the first press, a second press
inside the gap dropped, the next one not, a press on a heading silent, a toast
and a slide change each sounding once, a re-tap on the tab already shown sounding
not at all, and switching it back off going silent again.

The three shape fixes are asserted as **rules**, not as pixels — jsdom does not
lay out.

## Not verified

**No browser this session.** Everything visual here is unseen:

- the hours column — "they line up" is `margin-left:auto`, `text-align:right`,
  a floor width and tabular figures being present, and nothing more;
- the new gap under the sticky header, at both density extremes;
- `--r3` on four control shapes across fifteen themes, three of which set
  `--r-base` to 14–16px, where a "rounded square" is nearly a pill again;
- the tick box and the strike-through against the fifteen presets, and the
  label chips' 14% wash on the five light ones;
- **the sound itself, which no test can hear.** The harness proves the right
  graph is built at the right moment and torn down to silence; it says nothing
  about whether 660 Hz for 32 ms is pleasant, or whether the level dial's default
  is right on a phone speaker.

Worth an eye and an ear on a phone before this is trusted.

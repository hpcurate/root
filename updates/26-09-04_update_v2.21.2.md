## What was done

### Settings gets a new mark; DAY keeps the sun

The sun I gave DAY in 2.21 was a circle with eight radiating strokes. The
settings gear was a circle with eight radiating strokes. At 19px, four tabs
apart, they were the same mark — which is the entire failure of an icon.

The sun is the more appropriate of the two for an app about a day, so it stays
and **settings moved to sliders**: two rails with a knob on each, which is what
forty dials and a pile of toggles actually are. Each rail is drawn as two
segments with a gap where the knob sits, so nothing shows through a shape that
has no fill.

### The arrows are drawn, not typed

They were `←` and `→` as text in the mono face. A glyph is at the mercy of
whichever font is loaded, sits off the optical centre of its button, and
ignores the Icon weight dial that every other mark in the app follows.

They are `#ico-chev-l` / `#ico-chev-r` from the sprite now — **the same pair
the nav's own arrows have always used, at the same 17px** — so the three arrows
along that edge of the screen read as one family instead of as two icons and a
pair of characters.

## Verification

`cd root/test && node harness.mjs` — **417 checks, 0 failures**, 3 new.

The one worth keeping takes a **signature of every `tab-*` symbol's shapes**
and fails on a duplicate. The gear and the sun were both `circle+path`, so it
catches this class of mistake without anyone having to look at the tab bar —
which is the only way it was going to be caught, given I cannot see the bar.

Also: settings' symbol being lines and knobs rather than a circle and a ray
path, and the stepper's two buttons carrying `<use>` of the two chevrons with
no arrow character anywhere in their text.

## Deferred

Nothing. Though it is worth noting this is the second appearance bug in two
releases that only you could see — the harness now has shape-level checks for
both of them, but those check *distinctness*, not whether a mark is any good.

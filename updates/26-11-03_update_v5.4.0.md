## What was done

**The pets** (r261103-fz9m) — at the evening's end, a card each (`log.pets`: fixed keys `dog` and `cat`, free names — kamo and
kitten, settings → LOG → names and counts):

- its notes (ate well, the vet, a new trick…).
- The record keeps `e.pets = { dog: { play, notes }, cat: … }` (an empty one isn't kept). The day's note writes `#### pets` after
  curate output — `pet_dog_play`, `pet_dog_notes`, `pet_cat_…` — only on a day there is one, so older notes are untouched.

  his notes — "kitten" until he gives it one in settings.

**A few questions, and gratitude** (r261103-q3i0) — in the evening form after the pets, each optional:
- four questions (`log.questions`, his to reword): what did you do today? · what went well? · what was hard? · tomorrow's one
  thing? Each a line that grows as he writes.
- three lines "grateful for" (`log.gratitude`: 0–5).
- The day's note writes them under the journal's entries: `#### questions` (a question | answer table, the question as he was
  asked it) and `#### gratitude` (a line each) — only when answered, so a day with an entry and no answers reads as before, and a
  day with no entry now has words.
- The reports: "your answers | 3 days answered · 7 things you were grateful for"; the week's ends with its gratitude, each with
  its day.
- Three new switches in `log.fields` (settings → LOG → which fields appear): pets, questions, gratitude.

**The month's report as a file** (for r261103-ptto) — `LOG.monthReportFor(iso)`, a day's month the way `weekReportFor` gives its
week: `review_monthly_YY.MM.md`, the history's month share. CORE 0.43.0's ROOT window writes it into the vault's `entries/monthly`
with the day notes and the weeks (never over a file written in since), and CORE's monthly run adds its recap and guidance at its
end on the 1st.

## What was wrong

Two causes, both in `blocksOf()`, and one of them explains why the previous
three attempts each got partway.

**A `.view` has exactly two children.** Shell restructures every slide into the
title band and `.view-body`. So asking a *view* for its blocks answered "the
band, and everything else on the page" — and stepping into the second handed
back a flat list of every control on the screen, which is precisely what blocks
were added to replace. On a slide with no band it happened to descend further
and behave, which is why it half-worked on some tabs.

**And on DO's home the descent went one level too far.** `#s-home` has one child
with anything in it — the routine grid — so the "unwrap single children" rule
unwrapped it and returned its six cards as six blocks. Every block was a
**leaf**. `enterSel()` correctly refuses to step into a block holding one
control, so on the most-used screen in the app there was genuinely nothing to
enter. "The selector appears but I can't go into one block" was exact.

## What was done

**Blocks come from the open screen.** The band is one block; the rest are the
sections of whichever `.scr` is `.on`. And a lone child is unwrapped only when
it is a group *of groups* — a container whose children are the controls
themselves *is* the block. DO's home is now the band and the grid; entering the
grid walks its cards.

**`shown()` no longer asks `offsetParent`** — and this is the more important
half of the change.

`offsetParent` is null for anything `position:fixed`, so the numpad, the sheets
and the nav all read as hidden at exactly the moment they are the only thing on
screen. It is also null for *everything* under jsdom, which is why three
versions of this shipped without a single check that could move the cursor: the
part of the app that most needed testing was the one part the harness could not
touch, and I kept shipping reasoning instead of evidence.

The question is asked in ROOT's own vocabulary now — a `.scr` without `.on`, a
`.view` without `.cur`, `[hidden]`, `.hidden`, inline `display:none`. Those are
how this app actually hides things; the answer is more correct in a browser and
available in the harness.

**`Shell.cursor` is exposed for tests.** Nothing in the app calls it — the
keyboard does.

## Verification

`node test/update.mjs check` — 20 syntax checks, smoke across 10 apps / 15
themes / 14 panels, **1122 behaviour checks and 6 runner tests passed** (13
added, 1 rewritten).

For the first time the checks **drive the cursor** rather than reading its
source: what a screen's blocks actually are, that none of them is `.view-body`,
that the band and the grid are both among them, that the grid is one block
holding its cards rather than one block per card, that stepping in lands on the
first control, that the ring inside is bounded by the block, that coming back
out returns to the block you came from, and that leaving from the top level
reports false instead of pretending.

They are written against properties rather than counts, because the harness
shares state and earlier checks change how many routines DO has.

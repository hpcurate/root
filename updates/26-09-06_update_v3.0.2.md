## Request

**Source:** Hugo, directly in the session — not a Todoist task.

> replace the green/red animation to a + or - that appear for increase/decrease.
> in "store"

**Tab:** store.

## What was done

**The colour is gone.** STORE's running total went green on a rise and red on a
fall. It now puts a `+` or a `−` at the head of the number for a moment instead,
and no part of the counter changes colour at all.

Why a sign beats a colour here: a hue has to be decoded into a direction, and
this particular number already carries the accent in its shadow and sits under a
theme picker that can move the whole palette out from under it. A sign says the
same thing in one glyph and cannot collide with a preset.

**Where it sits.** At the head of the number, and `display:none` between changes
so it reserves nothing on a band with none to spare. The counter has the right
end of the row to itself (`justify-content:space-between`), so when the sign
appears the box grows *leftwards* — measured in Chrome: the box gained exactly
the sign's 28.4px and the digits did not move.

**What it wears.** What the number wears — title colour, accent shadow. Its job
is done by appearing, not by being another colour; 3.0.1 had just finished taking
the last two colour inversions out of this band and putting one back would have
been the same mistake a third time.

**Its whole life is one animation.** It turns in like one of the digit cells,
holds while the total is read, then fades; JS drops the class at the end of the
window. The glyph is `−` (U+2212), the typographic minus, which is a digit wide
in tabular figures — a hyphen would sit short and off-centre next to 54px digits.

**The nudge stays.** The number still moves a fraction of a millimetre up on a
rise and down on a fall. It is not a colour, it says the same thing the sign
says, and it was already there. Easy to drop if it is one signal too many — say
so and it goes.

## Verification

`test/harness.mjs` — **757 checks, all green.** Seven checks that asserted the
green/red flash were rewritten; five are new: the sign reserves nothing at rest,
a rise writes `+`, a fall writes `−`, no colour token is left anywhere in the
counter's rules, and the sign wears the number's treatment rather than one of its
own.

Driven in headless Chrome as well, at 820px where the counter has room:

```
counter fits      : yes (295/295)
sign              : "+"  display inline-block
box grew leftward : 28.4px      <- the digits keep their place
on a fall         : "−"
```

Screenshotted at 2× and looked at: `−25.00€`, one treatment across the sign, the
digits, the decimal point and the currency mark.

## Deferred / found on the way

**The pinned total does not fit beside `STORE.`, and this is not new.**

Measured in Chrome at 412×915 with Syne loaded:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| wordmark row | 376px |
| `STORE.` at the default title size | 326px |
| left for the counter | **36px** |
| what the counter needs | **267px** |
| cut off | **231px** |

It is clipped at every one of the five title sizes — even at `xs` it needs 193px
and gets 126px. `font-size:min(var(--title-px), 14vw)` never binds, because 14vw
at any phone width is bigger than 54px, so the cap that was supposed to make it
fit has never done anything. The 2.25.1 note claiming a four-figure total "still
fits beside `STORE.` at the largest title size" was never true.

This is a 2.25.1 defect rather than anything in this version, but it matters
here: **the sign added in this version is largely unseeable until it is dealt
with**, and anything added at the head of the counter makes the clipping worse
while it stands.

It is left open deliberately. Fixing it means making something materially
smaller while the counter is pinned — the wordmark, or the number — and that is
a decision about how the band looks rather than a bug fix. After the dot, a look
Hugo did not ask for is not a thing to hand him. The measurement is in ROOT.md §6
so it cannot be lost.

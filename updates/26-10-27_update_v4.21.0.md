## What was done

**LOG** (`js/log.js`, `css/log.css`)
- **The month as dots.** A day with both halves written is a dot in LOG's colour, one half is half a dot, and none is a
  ring. The date stays inside the dot. The selected day is ringed, and today has a point under its date. These rules come
  after the month's first rules and take over their look.
- **The fortnight as grouped bars.** Each day is one slot with its series side by side, each in its key's colour. A day
  with no reading has no bar, the same gap the line used to leave. When the chart is opened, the day labels sit under their
  slots. The key row still cycles the six charts.
- **The halves as cards.** Morning and evening are taller cards that say **"written"** once written. The half LOG's "!"
  is about floods in LOG's colour and says **"due"** with a big "!". This shows on today only, the same rule the tab's "!"
  follows.

**DAY** (`js/cal.js`, `css/cal.css`)
- **The current item is the sun card.** The row the clock is in is drawn in DAY's colour, on today only, never an empty
  slot and never a row that runs past midnight.
- The now line is the text colour, so it reads where it crosses that card.
- **Blocks by number:** a block's slot reads "block 2". Its label, `b2`, is unchanged, so Todoist and the 22:00 agent
  still see the same thing.
- Rows are a little rounder and names a little bigger.

Nothing either app stores, exports or sends changed: LOG's note and the vault files, DAY's record, the export and
`mode: schedule` are all untouched.

## What was done

### 2 — PLAN's transition, done as specified

Worth being blunt about the record here. 2.21 made the border fade faster. 2.22
made it faster again. 2.22.1 found that the tiles which *left* were never
animated at all and gave them ghosts. Each of those was a real improvement to a
thing that was still wrong, and the reason is that all three shared a wrong
assumption: that the fix was to animate *more* of the screen, better. It is not.
**A screen where eight things move at once has no subject, and a gesture with no
subject reads as a stutter however well each part is timed.** Your instruction
is the correct diagnosis and it is now what the code does.

`flip()` is rewritten around three groups, and every element on screen is in
exactly one of them:

- **the movers** — `.proj-name` and `.proj-dot`, the elements carrying
  `data-flip-text`, present in both snapshots. Translated and scaled from where
  they were to where they are going, over 58% of the motion budget.
- **the carrier** — the box the movers live in. It is *not* faded, because
  `opacity` on a parent takes its children with it and its children are the two
  things that must stay visible. Its **paint** is held transparent instead and
  animated in afterwards, so the box arrives with the rest of the screen rather
  than under the move.
- **everything else in the grid** — held at `opacity:0` for the whole move by
  `fill:'backwards'` on a delayed reveal, then brought in as a short wave, 22ms
  apart.

The queue below the grid is deliberately outside all three: it is not part of
the gesture, it just follows the grid's new height, so it slides as it always
did rather than blinking.

**The ghosts from 2.22.1 are gone.** What leaves is now simply absent on the
first frame — which is correct here and was not before: everything that *stays*
is invisible on that frame too, so the screen clears in one go instead of
half-cutting and half-fading.

Two things fell out of the rewrite that are worth knowing:

- **`data-flip-text` no longer subtracts.** It existed so the box's own slide
  could be taken off the text's, since both were moving. The box does not move
  any more, so the text carries its full delta; the attribute still names the
  carrier, which is what it is read for now.
- **There is no move at all when the name does not travel.** Opening a section's
  form leaves the heading exactly where it is. Holding the screen blank for
  400ms while nothing moves is a pause, not a transition, so `travels` decides
  and `moveMs` is 0 when it is false — there the reveal starts at once.

### 1 — a hairline round a day

`.lc-c` had `border:var(--bw) solid`. Forty-two of those in a dense grid is a
lattice, and the lattice was the loudest thing on the screen — louder than the
tints it was supposed to be framing. It is `max(.5px, calc(var(--bw) * .5))`
now: half the app's border weight, floored at .5px, which is one physical pixel
on any phone and so is the thinnest line that can still be drawn.

It is a **ratio of `--bw`, not a literal**, so the Border weight dial still
reaches it — §4's rule that a literal `1px` makes one surface immune to the
theme system applies here as much as anywhere.

The selected day's ring came down from 2px to 1.5px with it. It can afford to be
the one heavy line in the grid, because there is exactly one of it.

## Verification

`cd root/test && node harness.mjs` — **499 checks, all green.**

The scripted-layout transition test (jsdom has neither layout nor Web
Animations, so both are stood in for) was rewritten for the new shape:

- the tile is unpainted for the whole move and painted in after it, with a
  delay and `fill:backwards`, from `transparent`
- the name and its dot are the only things moving while it does
- the section rows are held back for the move, then revealed one after another,
  each starting no earlier than the tile's own reveal
- the queue outside the grid still slides, and is never blinked

Plus the source assertions, which is where the design decisions are pinned:
movers and carriers separated; a mover carrying its full delta and no longer
subtracting its box's; the carrier held unpainted rather than faded; everything
else in the grid held for the whole move; no move when nothing travels; no
`ghostOut` and no `position:relative` left on the grid; and the arithmetic that
move plus reveal still fits inside one `--t-flip`.

The hairline is asserted as a ratio of `--bw` and *not* as a literal
`var(--bw) solid`, and the selected ring at 1.5px.

Also run: `node --check` over all 13 JS files and a brace-balance count over all
12 stylesheets.

One check from 2.22 was **stale and is replaced**: "PLAN's border fade is a
fifth of the move" measured a crossfade that no longer exists. It now asserts
the opposite — that there is no crossfade under the move at all, which is the
point of the change.

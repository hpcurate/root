## What was done

### The icon-only wordmark

Two rules, each right on its own, wrong together once the letters are gone:

- `text-box:trim-both cap alphabetic` trims the wordmark's box to the **cap
  height** of its letters. That is what makes `DO.` sit optically centred beside
  the day number.
- `vertical-align:baseline` sits the mark on those letters' baseline.

With `font-size:0` there are no letters, no cap height and no baseline — so the
box collapsed to nothing and the mark hung off a baseline that was not where the
row's middle is. The gap between a cap height and a full box is exactly the
offset that was visible.

The fix is to stop laying it out as type at all in that mode: `text-box:normal`,
the wordmark becomes a flex box with `align-items:center` and a `min-height` of
the title size, and the mark becomes a `display:block` child of it. `name` and
`icon and name` are untouched — they still have letters, so the trim and the
baseline are still the right answer there.

### DATA reads the app

4.12 pointed LOG's charting idea at the three instruments beside it. The question
DATA is actually asked — *what do I do* — is a much bigger one than TOOLS' own
stores can answer, and this is that.

**Five families**, chosen so the chips answer a question rather than list the
apps:

| Family | Series |
| --- | --- |
| **body** | sleep, energy, mood, stress, kilometres walked |
| **done** | blocks, everything ticked on DAY, topics, cards rated, plants cared for |
| **made** | hours at the desk, what was watched |
| **tools** | focus minutes, breathing sessions, optimise runs |
| **life** | how much of the day got written, notes, caffeine, shops, spend |

Tapping a family narrows to it and offers its series individually.

**Every series is asked of the owning app**, never read out of its storage key:
`LOG.dayData`, `LOG.loggedDays`, `TRACK.doneOn`, `LEARN.dailyStats`,
`CREATE.dayStats`, `CAL.marks`, and two readers added for this —
`TEND.careOn` and `STORE.tripsOn`. That is the rule ROOT.md §6 already states
for LOG's note, and it is what keeps DATA from becoming a second definition of
every record in the app. Each call sits behind a `try` and each series declares
whether its app is present: an app switched off contributes nothing and the
chart carries on. The precedent is 4.1.1, where one undefined array in a cache
nobody validated took out a whole tab.

**`LOG.dayData` hands out a day, not the record.** Derived numbers only — sleep,
both energies, both moods, stress, kilometres, meals, caffeine, blocks, media,
entries, whether there was a workout or a cold shower, and `written`, which is
how much of the day exists at all. The record itself stays inside LOG, so a
caller cannot write through this and nothing here has to be kept in step with
the form. Morning and evening are both kept rather than folded together: the
day's shape *is* the pair, and averaging them here would throw it away.

**Two rules about honesty in the numbers**, both of which a chart gets wrong by
default:

- **An empty day is `null`, not 0.** A night nobody wrote down is not a night of
  no sleep. Nulls are dropped from every total, average and streak, so a blank
  day cannot pull a number towards nothing.
- **A rating is averaged and a count is totalled**, and the tile's caption says
  which. "27 mood" would be nonsense; so would "0.4 blocks a day" as a headline.

The bars now scale **each series against its own peak**, so kilometres and a
mood out of five can share one picture without either flattening the other, and
the pie is drawn only over countable series — a share of an average is not a
thing. The band's number counts every day anything in the app recorded, asked of
`LOG.loggedDays` rather than inferred.

A stored focus naming a series this install cannot draw — an app switched off
since, or a series a later build renames — falls back to everything rather than
to an empty screen that reads as a bug.

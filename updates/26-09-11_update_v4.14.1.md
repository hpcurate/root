## What was done

### The cause, which was not the merge

4.14.0 fixed how CREATE's record *merges*. That was a real fault and it is fixed,
but it was not this one: a practice session was never reaching the other device
to be merged in the first place.

**Todoist caps a task description at 16,384 characters.** Every non-day record
went into a single `ROOT · state` task. `do-stats-v1` on an install with a year
of routine history is about **36,000 characters on its own** — more than twice
the cap — so the API refused that task outright, and with it went everything else
riding in it: CREATE's sessions, TEND, the packing lists, DO's Todoist target.

The per-day tasks are small and kept working, which is exactly why it looked
like *some* things synced. Daily ticks arrived. The practice hours never left the
device.

Nothing in the module knew the limit existed.

### The fix

- **`stateTasks(body)`** splits the state across as many tasks as it takes —
  `ROOT · state`, `ROOT · state 2`, and so on — each under `DESC_MAX` (15,000;
  the margin is the prose above the fence and the fence itself). **One key never
  straddles two tasks**: a task is parsed on its own and half a record is worse
  than a missing one.
- **The reading side needed nothing.** `pullTodoist` already folds every
  `ROOT · …` task it finds into one payload.
- **`do-stats-v1` travels as its last 120 days** instead of all 400. The strip
  draws at most 60, and `mergeStats` only ever *fills in* a day the other side is
  missing — it never overwrites one — so an older day that stays at home stays
  correct on both devices.
- **A record too big for a task of its own is left behind and named** in the push
  result rather than taking the whole sync down with it. The toast says how many.
  A known limit beats a silent hole.
- **A leftover `state N` task** from a fatter payload is emptied rather than
  deleted — this module has never removed anything from Todoist, and an empty
  payload says the same thing without changing that.

`stateTasks` is pure and exported for one reason: the fault was never in the
merge, it was in what the transport could carry, and that is a thing a test can
measure.

## Verification

`node test/update.mjs check --full` — **1262 harness checks, 0 failed**, syntax
over 20 files, boot smoke, and the runner's own 6 tests. 11 checks are new, and
they are the ones that were missing:

- a 400-day tally is genuinely over the cap (the premise, asserted rather than
  assumed)
- the tally travels as a window, and a practice session is in the payload
- the state splits into tasks that are **all** within Todoist's 16,384
- every travelling key lands in exactly one task, and the practice session is in
  one that will actually be written
- folding the tasks back together gives the whole state again, session included
- a record too big for any task is left behind, named, and does not stop the rest

**Not verified:** the real round trip. The transports have always been outside
the harness — this needs two devices and the live API — so what is proven here is
that the payload now fits inside what Todoist accepts and reassembles correctly,
not that Todoist accepted it. That is the next thing to check on the two devices,
and it is the first version where it can succeed.

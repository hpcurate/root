## What was done

**Each dump, a Todoist task** (`quros/js/brain.js`, only inside ROOT, `?embed=root`):
- in `04 | core › brain | dumps` — the section is made the first time if it isn't there;
- its title the topic and the time ("Body › Sleep · 26-10-27 08:15"), its description the words, then one line
  `brain: {…}` with its record (id, times, length, words, language, topic) — the shape BRAIN's `core/memory.js` reads;
- a long one: the first 15 000 characters in the description, the rest in "brain: more" comments;
- it goes once it has been quiet three minutes (a dump carried on with "add more" goes whole), waits in a queue on the phone
  while offline or without ROOT's Todoist key, and is never sent twice;
- BRAIN takes it into Hugo's memory in Obsidian and closes the task.

**The map, one task** "brain · map": qurOS's markdown (its [x] / [~] states), updated a few seconds after the map or a state
changes. BRAIN reads it for the coverage (his answer 8: "the maps coverage").

**Hooks** in qurOS's own `saveEntry`, `setMap` and `setItemState`, and a "BRAIN" line in qurOS's settings: how many wait, when
the last went, a reason when one is stuck — never red.

The audio stays on the phone: a web page can't write to the Drive, so the PC's whisper.cpp works on the PC's recordings, and the
phone's words are its own transcript.

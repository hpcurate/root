## What was done

**Level 0, the home** (`js/settings.js` `renderHome`, `css/settings.css`):
- search;
- the four areas as **colour cards**, two by two: apps violet, appearance pink, sync green, data sun;
- a **quick** group: animation, texture, and the explanations under the controls.

On the dock, the home no longer lists the apps that are out of the bar; the R holds every app. Off the dock it still does.

**Level 1, an area** (`#s-area`, `SET.cat`): a category with more than one panel is a page of rows. These are the eleven
apps, each with its icon and what it is for, or look / layout / behaviour. The eleven-pill bar used to run off the phone.
Sync and data have one panel each and go straight to it.

**Level 2, a panel**: it is unchanged inside (its markup, its ids and its pill bar of siblings), with a **trail** above it
(settings › apps › do) where each step is a way back. **Back** (`SET.up()`) climbs one level at a time instead of
jumping home.

**Level 3, an app's content**: the content at the end of an app's panel is a list of rows, one per editor (daily routines,
media labels, packing…). A row opens **that one list alone**, and the rest of the panel steps aside (`SET.focus(path)`).
The editors stay in the page, folded, so a link or a search can open one straight away. Search's content hits now do,
landing on the list that holds the match.

**Kept:**
- every panel id and every `#settings/<panel>` link (CORE and ROOT desktop use them);
- the category buttons;
- `SET.panel` / `home` (`cat` now opens the area page).

## What was done

**STORE's sync** (`js/store.js`). It was a union where nothing was ever deleted or completed, so what was bought came back on the next sync. Every list item is now tied to its task (`tid`):
- **Tick = closed in Todoist, untick = reopened**, at once (like DO's ticks). With no connection the tick stays and the close or reopen waits in `todoist.queue` for the next sync; the opposite one still waiting cancels it. A task gone from Todoist (404) lets the item go: unticked, the next sync sends it again.
- **✕ deletes it in both**, with the undo pill to put it back (the same task if the delete is still waiting, a new one on the next sync if it went). Clearing the whole list still leaves Todoist alone (not asked).
- **Duplicates, with a margin** (`sameItem`): the same words once plurals are stripped (carrot / carrots, tomato / tomatoes, cherry / cherries), or one slip in a word of 6+ letters (one of them 7+) that starts the same (yogurt / yoghurt). Short words must match exactly: pasta / paste, butter / bitter, coffee / toffee stay two things. Adding one that's already on the list adds to it (tomato then tomatoes = tomatoes ×2). A sync merges what's left on both sides under the fuller name (`bestName`: carrots) and deletes the extra tasks.
- **Two-way by task**: an unticked item is its task (STORE's quantity, the fuller name); a task done or deleted in Todoist ticks the item here; one STORE closed and Todoist reopened is unticked here; an item ticked before it ever reached Todoist closes the task of the same name rather than being pulled back.
- Restoring a trip unticks through the same path, so a ticked item it brings back reopens its task.

**Claude's reading, open to change:** "complete" is the tick and "undo the complete" is the untick. A ticked item stays on STORE's list, struck through, until the trip is saved: the band's count and the trip's record need it. ✕ is the delete. If you meant ticked items to leave STORE's list at once, say so.

**Settings note** under STORE's Todoist target (`index.html`) says the new rule. `ROOT.md`: the 4.16 changelog, the `store_state_v1` row (§5: `tid`, `tdc`, `todoist.queue`), STORE's line in §1. `_prompts/ECOSYSTEM.md`'s "add-only" line updated.

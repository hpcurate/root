## What was done

**The rail of every app** (`css/shell.css`, `js/shell.js` `rebuild`): on a wide window the dock stands up as the rail, as
the pill did. It now lists **every app**, the dock's first and then the rest, followed by settings and the R. On a phone
the extra buttons stay hidden; they come after the dock's in the bar so the order reads right in both.

**Today** (`js/shell.js` `enterToday` / `leaveToday`), from 1100px, first in the rail:
- It puts **DO, DAY, LOG and TOOLS side by side**, each in its own column with its own band, scroll and colour.
- They are **the apps themselves**, not pictures of them: a routine ticks in DO's column, DAY's current block is its sun
  card, LOG's due half is pink, and the breathing round or the timer runs in TOOLS'.
- Any move to one app ends today, and so does a window that becomes too narrow. A today left open is where the next start
  lands (`#today` links to it), and the swipe is off while it is up.

**Arriving on an install** (marker `root5wide`): `desktopMode` now ships as `rail`. An install on the phone-shaped frame,
the old default, moves to it once. A phone looks the same either way, and so does ROOT desktop's usual 460px window: the
difference is only at full width. The frame is still one tap away under settings → layout.

**Not built from the plan's phase 8:** LOG's big month with all six charts at full width, and settings' levels as
columns. At full width those still read as the phone's pages, widened.

## What was done

- **The cause** (`css/plan.css`, 5.1.1's chips): a section chip's colour dot was sized only by `flex:0 0 8px`. A chip that is as
  wide as what it holds counts its children by their width, and the dot's width was 0, so the name was given 8px less than it
  needed and lost its end. Measured in Electron: "mixing" 35 of 43px, "production" 63 of 71, "socials" 42 of 50.
- **The fix:** the dot has its own `width:8px`, the name `flex:0 1 auto`, the chip `max-width:100%`. Each name shows whole; only
  a name longer than the row itself is cut. The project chips and the "other projects" chips already had their dots' widths.
- Claude's reading: "the categories" are the open project's sections (the chips under curate). The project chips themselves
  weren't cut.

## What was done

- **Hold to unlock** (`quros/js/rec.js`): the locked recorder's slider is a button held for 1.5 s (`HOLD`). It fills as it is
  held, with a short vibration when the hold starts and the usual one when it unlocks; lifting the finger or sliding off
  empties it. Nothing else unlocks it, as before (keys, the back gesture, other touches).
- **Clear of the dock:** inside ROOT the recorder's foot — the four buttons and the hold — is lifted by `--bar-h` (the dock's
  height plus 30, `js/quros.js`), so it ends above the dock's strip instead of in it (`quros/css/app.css`).
- Settings' "lock when talking starts" says hold. QUROS.md §4 and its changelog; ROOT.md.

Why the dock hid it: the slider sat exactly where the dock is. ROOT already makes the dock transparent while recording, so
headless Chrome didn't show it; on a phone, a slide along the bottom edge also meets the phone's own swipe gestures. A hold
just above the dock avoids both.

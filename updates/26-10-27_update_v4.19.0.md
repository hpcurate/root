## What was done

**The dock** (`navStyle: 'dock'`, now the default; `css/shell.css`): the bar across the bottom of the phone.
- Its apps are a new list, `Prefs.dock`: do · log · day · tools to start, **five at most**, in your order.
- The app you are on widens and says its name beside its icon.
- The prev/next arrows are gone. A sub-screen's back button floats above the dock's left end while one is up.

**The R** (`#nav-r`, the dock's last button; `#r-sheet`, `js/shell.js`) opens every app and settings as a grid, each in
its own colour.
- The dock's apps carry a dot. An app's "!" or count comes over from its tab.
- The box finds an app by name, and past the apps offers "search everything for …", which opens search with the words
  already in (`SEARCH.open(q)`).
- **edit the dock** opens settings → layout at the dock's list. There, under the dock, the bar's editor edits the dock:
  on/off, ↑↓, and an app switched on joins the dock's end.
- An app opened from the R comes and goes exactly as an app out of the bar always has (`Shell.open`), and the R is lit
  while you are on it or on settings. Escape and the backdrop close the sheet, and a swipe under it is refused.

**Getting around:**
- **The swipe** walks the dock's apps and **stops at its end** (his answer 3). From an app opened from the R it still
  steps back.
- `Shell.go()` now falls back to `open()` for an app with no tab, so every link and module that goes to PLAN or STORE
  still gets there.
- A last tab that is in the R now reopens on boot.

**Arriving on an install** (marker `root5dock`): an install on the floating pill, the old default, moves to the dock once.
A bottom bar or a hidden bar was a choice and stays. The other three styles read `apps` as before.

**Layouts:** in the frame (the phone-shaped box, ROOT desktop's default) the dock stays inside it. A wide window in rail
mode keeps the rail until phase 8 (the desktop).

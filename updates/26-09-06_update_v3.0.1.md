## What was done

### The root cause, which is one line

`tokens.css` had `--title-sh:var(--title-sh-x) 0 0 var(--title-sh-c)` on
`:root`. **A custom property's own `var()` references are substituted when it is
computed — on the element it is declared on.** So `var(--title-sh-c)` resolved
to the accent *at `:root`*, and every element below inherited a finished string
with no property left in it to override.

**Every `--title-sh-c` override in the app was a no-op**, and had been since
2.25.1: the wordmark's dot, STORE's currency mark, and the green/red flash on
the running total.

Lengths behave the opposite way — they stay as tokens and resolve against
whoever uses them — which is why the offset *was* correctly 3px on the 54px
wordmark and 1px on the 0.4em currency mark. Half the token worked. That is what
kept it hidden.

**Fix:** there is no composed `--title-sh` any more. The shadow is written out
at the point of use, `text-shadow:var(--title-sh-x) 0 0 var(--title-sh-c)`, in
all twelve places that wear it — the only form where the element's own colour is
the one that counts. A check fails if any stylesheet reaches for the old token
again.

### The dot

**Title text colour, accent shadow — exactly like the letters in front of it.**

2.26.2 and 3.0 both tried to make the dot the *inverse* of the word (accent
glyph, title-coloured shadow). 2.26.2's attempt did nothing because of the
token; 3.0's attempt re-declared `text-shadow` on the `em`, which finally
applied an effect **nobody had asked for** — and left it further from what was
wanted than the version that did nothing.

Hugo asked three times for the same thing and the third time spelled it out:
*"accent shadow, title text colour"*. That is the word's own treatment. So the
special-casing is deleted rather than corrected — the `em` rule is gone from
`shell.css` and `color:var(--y)` is gone from all ten app sheets. The dot
inherits both, and there is nothing left to invert.

### STORE's total

With the token fixed, the digits, the decimal point, the shadow and the currency
mark all move to green or red together and ease back together. The currency mark
also stops being the inverse of the number beside it, for the same reason the
dot did: the total is one number and one signal, and half of it in the other
colour is what reads as a fault rather than as a total moving.

## Verification

**Measured in a real browser, not inferred from the stylesheet.** v3.0.0 shipped
two shadow fixes argued entirely from CSS text, because jsdom does not cascade
and a regex on a stylesheet will happily assert the half that does nothing.
This one was found and confirmed by launching headless Chrome over the devtools
protocol and reading `getComputedStyle` off the live page — about sixty lines
using node's global `WebSocket`, nothing installed.

```
             colour               shadow
before  dot  rgb(167,139,250)     rgb(167,139,250)    accent on accent — invisible
after   dot  rgb(222,222,222)     rgb(167,139,250)    title on accent — as asked

before  total (mid-flash) red     rgb(167,139,250)    the shadow never moved
after   total (mid-flash) red     the same red        linked, frame for frame
after   total (at rest)   --tx    accent              back where it started
```

At rest and mid-flash, `.h-cost`, all five digit cells and the currency mark
report the **same** `rgb()` for both colour and shadow colour. The wordmark was
also rendered at 3× and looked at: `DO.` reads as three title-coloured glyphs
each carrying a violet offset copy.

`test/harness.mjs` — **755 checks, all green.** Nine checks that had been
asserting the old token were rewritten to assert the behaviour instead, plus two
new guards: nothing in any stylesheet reaches for `var(--title-sh)`, and no
sheet paints the dot a colour of its own.

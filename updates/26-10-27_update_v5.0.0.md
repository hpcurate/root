## What was done

**qurOS itself, inside ROOT.** A copy of qurOS 2.0.1 (from `github/qurOS` 4a2b6e4) now lives in `root/quros/`. ROOT's new
qurOS tab opens it in a frame on the same site, with `?embed=root`.
- It is not re-implemented: the records, the recorder (lock, slider, screen kept on), the map, the export for the qurOS
  chat and every screen are qurOS's own code.
- ROOT and qurOS share an origin, so the tab reads and writes **the same records qurOS always has** (IndexedDB `qurOS`, the
  `qos_*` keys). Nothing is moved or migrated, and both apps show the same dumps for as long as the old one is kept.

**Embed mode** in the copy (`quros/js/app.js` `EMBED`, `quros/css/app.css`):
- no DAY tab (ROOT has its own, and PLAN's goals since 4.23), and a stored DAY tab lands on dump;
- the bar is a strip at the top of the frame (dump · map · send), so nothing sits on ROOT's dock;
- its big title gives way to ROOT's band, keeping the date line and the settings gear;
- **ROOT's colours on its tokens:** the ground, the surfaces, the text, and qurOS's tab colour as its accent, re-read when
  ROOT's look changes;
- room for the dock under its last line and toast, no scrollbars, no service worker.

**ROOT's side** (`js/quros.js`, `css/quros.css`, `index.html`, Prefs, themes, settings):
- **The tab:** qurOS is the twelfth app, with its own icon, its colour in every tab palette, a band reading **qurOS.**
  (its capitals kept under ROOT's lowercase titles), and a row in settings → apps whose button opens qurOS's own settings.
- **Loading:** the frame loads the first time the tab is shown, not at ROOT's start, since qurOS's offer to recover a
  dump belongs to opening qurOS.
- **While recording:** qurOS's recorder goes full screen, as on its own. While it is open, ROOT's dock, arrows and band
  step aside, so a locked recorder takes no touch but its slider. The frame allows the microphone, full screen, the screen
  wake lock, the clipboard and sharing.
- **In the dock** (his answer): do · log · day · **qurOS**. A dock still exactly as 4.19 shipped it takes qurOS in tools'
  place once (`root5quros`); a changed dock stays. Tools is in the R.
- Settings' storage report has a qurOS group (`qos_*`). Search, the R and settings name it "qurOS".

**ROOT 5 is complete:** the preset, the dock and the R, DO, LOG and DAY, settings in levels, PLAN's goals and the other
apps, the desktop's rail and today, and qurOS.

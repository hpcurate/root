## What was done

### 1 — nothing is "pressed" while a slide is scrolling

The square is `:active`. On a touch screen the browser applies it the moment the
finger lands and does not clear it until the finger lifts — so a touch that
turns into a *scroll* lights up whatever row it started on and keeps it lit for
the whole gesture, sliding under your finger. It is not specific to the quick
cards; every list in every app does it, and the quick cards are simply where you
happened to notice.

The fix is one token rather than one patch:

- The 22 press washes that said `:active{background:var(--s2)}` across five
  stylesheets now say `var(--press)`, and `--press:var(--s2)` is declared once
  in `:root`.
- The shell raises `data-scrolling` on `<html>` while any slide is moving — on
  every `scroll` event, and on any `touchmove`, so the first few pixels of a
  drag are covered too — and lowers it 160ms after the last one.
- `[data-scrolling="on"]{--press:transparent}` takes the wash out for the length
  of the gesture.

That reaches every `:active` in the app at once, and any press rule added later
gets it for free. **A tap that is a tap still lights up**, because a tap does not
scroll — the feedback is not removed, only the false positive.

**One thing left alone, deliberately:** the rules that press with a *project's*
colour (PLAN's tiles, DO's block tiles) mix their own hue and have a different
resting value per rule, so they cannot share one token without duplicating each
rule. They are not inside long scrollers, so this has not been worth it. Noted
in ROOT.md §6 so it is a decision rather than an oversight.

### 2 — the quick section folds from its title

Tapping **quick** hides the cards and leaves the head. The count stays readable
while it is folded, so the section still tells you there are three things
waiting, and the way back is the thing you just tapped. That is deliberately
different from switching the section off in settings, which is permanent and
lives somewhere else.

The title had to keep looking like the heading it replaced — a heading that is
also a button is only obvious the first time, and after that it is just the
heading — so the only tell is a small caret that rotates. The state is
`quickFold` in `do_todoist_v1`, so it survives a reload.

### 3 — the fortnight opens over the month

Tapping the chart puts it where the calendar was; tapping it again gives the
calendar back. The key row says `14d` when it is small and `close` when it is
open, so there is no hidden state.

What the extra room buys is **axes**, which is most of what makes a chart
answerable: a line that rose is worth much less than a line that rose from 2 to
4 on the ninth. Open, it carries a 1–5 scale down the left, the day of the month
under every point, and gridlines at each step.

Two decisions worth recording:

- **The axes are HTML, not SVG `<text>`.** The plot uses
  `preserveAspectRatio="none"` so a fortnight always fills the width whatever
  the phone — which means anything drawn inside the viewBox is stretched with
  it, and a label 1.4× as wide as it is tall is a label you have to work at.
  They are absolutely positioned `<span>`s instead, each at the same fraction
  the plot draws with, so they line up **by construction** rather than by eye.
- **The plot gained a 4% inset at each end.** Half of the first and last dot
  used to hang over the edge of the box, and the x labels need somewhere to sit.

The grow is small on purpose (`scale(.97)` → 1, over `--dur-3`). It is the same
object gaining room, not a panel arriving, and a big scale would say the
opposite.

### 4 — two motion dials

**Animation speed**, 0.5× to 3×. `--mo` used to be one variable that the Motion
preset overwrote; it is `--mo-base` (the preset — an attribute) times
`--mo-scale` (the dial — an inline property) now. Split for two reasons: so the
two **compose**, since "reduced, and half again" is a real thing to want and
setting the dial should not throw away the preset's character; and because a
*speed* runs the opposite way to a *duration*, so the pref stores what the label
says (a speed) and `Prefs.apply()` writes `1/speed`. Everything that multiplies
by `--mo` — every transition, the tab cross-fade, the title morph, PLAN's
`--t-flip` — follows automatically.

**Keep the bar moving**, a toggle that only does anything at Motion: none. The
pill growing into place under the tab you picked is feedback rather than
decoration: with it gone, the only confirmation that the tap landed is that the
page has already changed, which is not the same thing. It is one scoped
exception to the blanket `[data-motion="none"]` rule — `#nav`, its children and
`.nav-arrow`, nothing else.

**A decision made on your behalf:** it is **off** by default. Someone who sets
motion to none has asked for none, and should get none until they say otherwise;
the toggle is there to make the exception, not to have it made for them.

  14 x labels, 5 gridlines; those placed at 0/25/50/75/100% and 4%…96%, the same
  fractions the plot uses, and strictly increasing across; they are HTML and
  there is no `<text>` in the svg; the three series still draw and the key says
  "close"; tapping again restores the month and the key says "14d".
- **Motion:** `--mo` composing from `--mo-base` × `--mo-scale` and the preset
  overriding the base rather than `--mo` itself; speed 2× writing `--mo-scale`

  `data-nav-motion="off"`; switched on, the attribute set and the exception in

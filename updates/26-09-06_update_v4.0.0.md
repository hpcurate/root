## What was done

### CREATE now holds areas

`js/config.js`, `js/create.js`. `create.areas` is an ordered list; each entry is
a `key`, a `label`, a `noun` and `plural`, a `color`, the `kinds` its sessions
are called, what a new one is pre-filled with, and the `stages` it walks. Two
ship: **production** (the six song stages as before) and **mixing**.

Nothing in `create.js` is written for two areas — it walks `AREAS`, whatever is
in it. A harness check greps the module for the literal `production` or `mixing`
outside a comment and fails if either appears. A third area is a block in Config
and no code.

**mixing's stages** are a DJ's, not a mix engineer's: `crate` → `order` →
`drill` → `record` → `played` (terminal), each with its own checklist. Its
session chips are *digging · practice · recording · playing out · listening
back*.

**Decision made on Hugo's behalf:** production's `mix` stage is now **labelled**
"mixdown". Its key stays `mix` — every tick ever filed is under it — but next to
an area called mixing the old label read as the same thing, which is the exact
confusion this version exists to remove. ROOT.md §6 says so, so it does not get
tidied back.

### The home page, redone as one combined shelf

`index.html`, `js/create.js`, `css/create.css`. A pill per area with `all` in
front of them, and `all` is the default. On `all`: one stage strip per area
stacked, the two lists together with each row wearing a dot in its area's
colour, and one add button per area. Picking an area narrows the list, the strip
and the add button together, and is remembered.

The week's hours are split by area under the three tiles — hours only, and the
bars are relative to the biggest rather than to the total, because the question
is which of the two got the time. The session log carries the same filter and
totals per area.

**Decision made on Hugo's behalf:** "a combined view and then a per area view"
is built as one screen with a filter rather than as two screens. On a phone the
alternative is a dashboard you tap through to reach the thing you wanted, and
the combined view stops being the one you actually use.

### "In flight" is gone

Not only from the heading: the section is **In progress**, the fold is
**Finished** rather than **Released**, and the module, the sheet and the markup
are all checked for the old phrase.

### The record: v1 → v2, lifted on read

`create_v1` now holds `works` (each with an `area`) instead of `songs`, ticks
are filed under `areaKey|stageKey|item text` instead of `stageKey|item text`,
and sessions carry the area they went into. A pre-4.0 record is lifted by
`normalise()` on read — every song becomes a work in the first area, every tick
key gains its area segment, every session follows its work — and `load()` writes
the lifted record straight back.

It runs in the reader rather than behind a repair flag because reading is
idempotent, and a migration that runs on read cannot be skipped by an install
that never opens Settings.

**A real bug, caught by its own test.** The lift first read the record shape off
the object already merged onto `blank()`. `blank()` supplies an empty `works`,
so "does this record have a `works`?" always answered yes and a v1 shelf was
read as an empty v2 one — silently deleted. It reads off the raw parse now, and
ROOT.md §6 carries it, because it is the shape of mistake any future migration
here can make.

### The editor, and search

`js/settings.js`, `js/search.js`, `css/settings.css`. `create.stages` and
`create.sessionKinds` are replaced by one `create.areas` editor that edits the
tree: an area's fields, then its stages nested inside it. `+ add a stage`
carries the area whose button it is; the button at the bottom adds a whole area;
the last area cannot be deleted. The generic editor dispatcher now passes
`data-add` through to `add()` — every other editor ignores the argument.

Search indexes the whole tree, and every row says which area it belongs to,
since a stage label is ambiguous across areas by design.

### `test/peek.mjs`

A second tool beside the harness: it prints a screen as words — what is on it,
in what order, what it reads like. jsdom has no layout so it cannot judge size;
it answers the half of "did that come out right" that does not need eyes, which
is the half still available with no browser. It is what caught `mix` sitting
next to `mixing` on a shelf row.

### The manifest

lift's trap, and the `mix`/mixdown label), and the 4.0 changelog entry.

## Deferred

Nothing was left unbuilt.

Not built because it was not asked for, and worth saying so: mixing has no
crate of individual tracks and no harmonic matching. A mix here is one piece of
work walking stages, which is what "make it function like CREATE" asked for. If
the per-track side is wanted later it is a different object and probably a
different app.

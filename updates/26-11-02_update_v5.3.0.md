## What was done

**The kinds** (`js/walk.js` `KINDS`, `shape`; a 2×2 row above "how long"):
- **loop**: as before, three points on a circle through the start.
- **far loop**: a diamond three times as long as it is wide, out by one way and back by another — about half its length away
  from the start, against a loop's third. His "go far but it doesn't have to be a mega loop".
- **out & back**: the router asked one way only, the line walked home the same way — half its length away, the farthest a walk
  that comes home can go. The spurs are cut from the way there before it is doubled (cut from the whole, the whole would go).
- **one way**: ends somewhere else, all its length away.
- Each is sized as the loop is: a first guess from the shape's length, corrected by what the router measures, within 7%.
- Under the map, **"km out"**: how far it gets from the start, as the crow flies. It takes "start"'s place (four numbers don't fit
  WALK's face on a phone; the from row says where it starts) — Claude's call.
- The band, the button and the toasts name the kind ("a far loop for kamo", "another walk"). Google's directions: out & back through
  its far end only, one way to where it ends.

**The link** — `#walk/go?kind=far&min=60` (also `km=`, `from=here|home`): the dials take what it says, a walk is found as the
button finds one, the address goes back to `#walk` (a reload never asks again). The shell's boot writes `#walk` over the address
before the apps load, so it now keeps the one it opened on (`Shell.landed()`); an open page reads the link on `hashchange`.

**Storage:** `walk_v1` gains `kind` (missing = loop, as every loop before was). ROOT.md: the WALK row, `walk_v1`'s row, the
changelog.

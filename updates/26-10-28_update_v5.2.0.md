## What was done

**The starter map** (`quros/js/model.js` `STARTER`): BRAIN 0.2.0's questions — 14 areas (Me, People, Pets, Home and daily
life, Body and mind, History, Education, Work and income, Money, Admin, Music, Systems and tech, Taste, Possessions), 73
sections — generated by BRAIN's `core/life.js` with `{ generic: true }` and pasted in, so its keys are BRAIN's. It has **no
one's name** in it (the old starter had his partner's): ROOT is a public site. His own map, with the people, places and
things of his life, only ever comes through Todoist.

**The map from the PC** (`quros/js/brain.js` `pullMap`): BRAIN takes the phone's marks from the task "brain · map", then
writes its own map there with `brain: {"kind":"map","from":"pc","at":…,"parts":n}` (the rest of a long one — his is 15 000
characters — in "brain: map n" comments). On each flush, when nothing of the phone's map is waiting to go (at most every two
minutes), a newer map from the PC is joined in order and loaded **as the PC's** (`Q.m.loadMapText(text, { from: 'pc', at })`):
its marks are the answer for its items (BRAIN took the phone's before it wrote them), other states stay, and it is never
sent back. A part not there yet waits for the next look; a change made on the phone meanwhile goes first. The phone's own
map now says `"from":"phone"` and when it was sent.

**Where it shows:** the map page says "from BRAIN on the PC, <when>"; settings' BRAIN line says when the map came.

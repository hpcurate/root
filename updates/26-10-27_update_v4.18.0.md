## What was done

**A preset, ROOT** (`js/prefs.js`, `css/themes.css`): the default and the first dark theme. The redesign's palette:
- a violet-cast black ground (#0b0a0f) with surfaces #15131b / #1c1924 / #242030;
- text #f1edf7 / #b3adc2;
- the accent violet; green #3ddc84 and sun #ffc94d as `--gr` and `--or`.

Its character is Syne over JetBrains Mono, radius 14, flat, and **grain on** (answer 7).
- **Its ground, three depths deep** (new pref `ground`: dark / darker / black, darker by default; only ROOT has the ramps).
  It sits in settings → look under the themes while ROOT is on.
- **Each app in its colour.** Under ROOT, the shipped tab palette is ROOT 5's five shape colours:

  | colour | apps |
  | --- | --- |
  | violet | do, create |
  | pink | log, learn |
  | sun | day, store |
  | green | plan, tend, walk |
  | lavender | track |
  | chalk | tools, settings |

  Through the per-tab accent ROOT already had, each app wears its own. Every one of them takes the ground's black as its
  ink: the palette names it per tab, and `tabAccent` now honours a palette's own `--tab-<app>-ink`. The luminance cut
  would have put white on violet and pink. Every other palette and theme is unchanged.
- **The band** (`css/themes.css`, under ROOT only): the wordmark lowercase, 60px (the title dial still scales it), no hard
  shadow. Its dot is in the app's own colour, set per slide so a swipe doesn't repaint the slide on its way out. The band
  keeps room under the descenders, because it clips sideways for the title slide.
- **The press:** a card and a band action give under the finger and spring back. This uses `scale`, not `transform`, so
  nothing that positions itself with a transform moves.
- **DO's strip** keeps its three tabs in view beside the bigger wordmark (tighter chips under ROOT).

**Arriving on an install that already has a theme:** Prefs stores every key, so a new default only reaches a fresh
install. An install still on **Void**, the old default, moves to ROOT once (for the system pair's dark half too), marked
`root5`. Void picked afterwards stays picked, and any other theme was a choice and stays.

**The light themes folded away** (answer 1, `js/settings.js`, `css/settings.css`): under "More themes · light", below the
dark ones. The fold is open while a light theme is the one on. "Follow the system" still offers all of them.

**Fixed on the way** (`js/shell.js` `fitNum`): the big number opposite the wordmark was fitted to the row minus the
wordmark only. DAY's row also holds its date, and with ROOT's bigger title the day number ran off the phone's edge. The
fit now takes what everything else in the row leaves.

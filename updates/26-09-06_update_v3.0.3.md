## What was done

Measured in Chrome first, which turned up **two** separate causes for one gap.

### 1. The titles centred their line boxes, not their letters

A wordmark is all caps, and caps in a `line-height:1` box leave the descender
space empty underneath — `STORE.` is **35.1px of ink in a 54px box**. The band
had no slack to redistribute either: 14px top padding + 82px of content + 12px
bottom padding was *exactly* its 108px floor, so `justify-content:flex-end` had
nothing to do and all of that empty space sat under the title.

- the titles carry `text-box:trim-both cap alphabetic`, so their boxes **are**
  the caps — the wordmark, every sticky sub-screen `.hd-title`, and STORE's total
- the band is `justify-content:center`
- its floor is measured in the trimmed title rather than the untrimmed one

Every band went from **108px to 94px**, and what is centred is what you can see.

The day number is the one thing that cannot be trimmed with `text-box`: its
digits are absolutely positioned so one can slide out while the next slides in,
and an absolute child has no line box to trim. It is given `--title-cap * 1em` by
hand and its digits are pulled up by the half-leading it no longer has, so it
still sits on the wordmark's baseline.

### 2. STORE was adding a second gap under its own band

§6 has said since 2.19 that *the gap under the band is the shell's, and an app
must not add to it*. The rule zeroes the first child's top **margin** — and
STORE's `.cnt` was **padding**, on all four sides. Every other app's `.cnt` is
horizontal padding only.

So on STORE's home the counter widget sat 18px from the shell plus 18px of
STORE's own: 36px. That is the gap between the title card and the calculator,
exactly as reported. Wordmark to widget is **32px now rather than 56px**.

It is fixed as one rule (`.ns-store #s-home > .cnt{padding-top:0}`) rather than a
blanket "zero the first child's padding": LEARN's import drop-zone is 22px of
padding that is the control's own shape, not a gap, and a sweep would have
deformed it.

### 3. One row height for every band

Trimming the wordmark had a consequence worth naming. DO's tab rail is 40px once
its border and its density-scaled padding are counted, and the moment the title
stopped being 54px tall that rail became the tallest thing in the row — making
DO's band 2px taller than the other nine.

One band shape for every app is what makes the title morph read as one title
being pushed along, so:

- the row is `max(--band-row, trimmed title)` for **every** app
- DO's rail is pinned to `--band-row`, so density cannot push one band out of
  step with the rest — which is what §6's "anything sharing the wordmark's row
  needs a fixed height" was always asking for and never actually enforced

All ten bands are 94px, at every title size.

### 4. The counter travels with the titles

`.h-cost` joins `.h-logo`, `.hd-title` and `.h-daynum` in the morph: it slides in
from the side you are moving towards while the outgoing one leaves the other way,
instead of appearing where the last one left it. Reduced motion takes it off with
the rest of the morph.

### Tokens

Two new, both documented in §4 and neither a dial:

- `--title-cap: .65` — Syne's cap height as a fraction of the em, **measured**
  rather than guessed. Only ever sizes boxes around trimmed text, so a different
  display face is one number rather than a hunt.
- `--band-row: 40px` — the wordmark's row, and the floor under it.

**A note on one of the new checks.** The first version of "the band centres its
content" was written with `[\s\S]*?` and matched a `justify-content` three rules
further down — the exact trap §6 warns about, and it was caught by the check
failing rather than by luck. All five lazy matches in this batch are bounded with
`[^}]` now.

## What was done

**The hero** (`js/do.js` `renderHome`, `css/do.css`): over the routine tiles, in DO's accent.
- The tab's ticks out of all its items (20 / 29) and "N of M done".
- One segment per routine, lit once it is finished.
- It counts every routine on the tab, including ones the "hide finished" dial has hidden.

**Each routine a tile:**
- Its name as you wrote it, in the display face, then its count, then what is next to tick.
- A **shape** fills from the bottom in the routine's **colour** as you tick. The shapes are disc, quarter, dome, square
  and bars; the colours are violet, green, pink, sun and chalk.
- A finished tile **floods** with its colour. It says "✓ closed in todoist" when DO's own record (`td.closedOn`) says
  Todoist took the close today, and "✓ done" otherwise.
- Travel is a chalk tile on its tab.
- The minimal card keeps its one-row shape and its bar.

**The routine** (`#s-checklist`): it opens on a **pie** in its colour with the count in the middle. The rows are 58px tall
with round checks in the routine's colour, and a ticked row is muted and struck through.

**Shape and colour are his** (answer 8, `js/config.js`, `js/settings.js`):
- They are two new optional fields on each routine in Config, `shape` and `color`. The shipped six carry the mockup's:

  | routine | shape | colour |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | fix | disc | violet |
  | fit | quarter | green |
  | log | dome | pink |
  | eat | disc | sun |
  | reset | square | chalk |
  | plan | bars | violet |
  | deep clean | square | green |

- A routine without them, like an override stored before 4.20, takes the one its key gives, or its place in the list
  (`DO.lookOf`).
- They are set in settings → do → routines: two selects under each routine's name, saved with the rest.
- The fields are added, never renamed: an old export still loads, and sync carries them with the branch.

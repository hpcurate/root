## What was done

**LOG** (`js/log.js`): `#log/now`, `#log/morning`, `#log/evening`.
- `now` picks by the hour: from 15:00 the evening; before, the morning — or LOG's home once the morning is written
  (`LOG.linkHalf`). Always today's; a day picked in the band is put back first.
- Never over a form with unsaved words: the link is dropped and the form stays (`LOG.linkAsk`).
- Afterwards the address is plain `#log`.

**brain** (`js/quros.js`): `#quros/talk`, `#quros/write`.
- The brain tab opens with qurOS's recorder up and ready (`Q.rec.ready`, what qurOS's own `?go=talk` shortcut does), or its
  writing sheet. The frame is loaded first when it isn't, and asked until qurOS has booted (9 s at most). Never over an open
  recorder. Afterwards the address is plain `#quros`.

Both are read the way WALK's link is (5.3.0): the shell goes to the tab by the name before the slash; the app reads the rest
from `Shell.landed()` at the start and from `hashchange` after.

**Decisions made on his behalf:**
- **15:00 is where the day turns** to the evening half (LOG's own alerts use 10:00 and 21:00 for "nothing recorded yet"; a tap
  at 16:00 means the evening). Before 15:00 with the morning written, LOG's home: there is nothing to fill in yet.
- **The recorder is ready, not recording**: a web page can't start the microphone without a tap, and qurOS's own shortcut does
  the same.

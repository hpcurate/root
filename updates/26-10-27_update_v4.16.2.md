## What was done

**The number's box** (`css/shell.css`, `js/shell.js`): the band's `.h-daynum` (LOG's and DAY's date, CREATE's and TOOLS' counts).
- **Why it clipped:** the box was sized for two narrow digits (`min-width: 1.1em`) and its digits were absolutely positioned against its right edge, then clipped sideways (`clip-path`, so the shuffle can't spill). A count past 99, like DATA's days on record, ran out of the box to the left and lost its first digit.
- **Now the live number is in the flow**, so the box is as wide as its digits. Only the leaving one (the shuffle's `.dn-out`) is lifted out. The shadow's pixel is reserved by the box's `padding-right`, as before, so it isn't cut either.
- **It never pushes the wordmark out of the row.** Where the number hasn't the room beside the wordmark, `Shell.rollNum` sets it smaller (`--dn-fit`) and drops it onto the wordmark's baseline. The wordmark is never touched, because every slide's band has the same size. A ResizeObserver on the row and the wordmark refits it when the phone turns, the title size changes or the font arrives.
- **Claude's call:** the smallest it gets is 9px, the size of the label above it, which says what it counts. In the default display font, "TOOLS." leaves only about 20px on a 390px-wide phone. There a three-digit count is that small; at 430px it is about 18px. Neither is cut.

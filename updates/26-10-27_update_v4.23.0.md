## What was done

**Goals on PLAN's home** (`js/plan.js` `renderGoals`, `css/plan.css`, `index.html` `#plan-goals`), above the projects:
- **today**, three at most, and **this week** (Monday to Sunday), three at most;
- each added from its own field with Enter and removed with ✕, the field going once there are three;
- a goal is a pill with a round tick: ticked, it is struck through and stays; nothing is counted, no streak and no score;
- what a **passed day or week left open** is offered once, under its row: **carry** (into the new one, while there is room)
  or **drop**, and then it is not offered again;
- they redraw whenever PLAN is shown and when the day turns, so tomorrow's PLAN starts on tomorrow's three.

**Storage:** a new key, `plan_goals_v1`. It is local, and sync carries it in its apps group like any new key. Nothing goes
to Todoist, and nothing else PLAN stores changed.

**Not in this version, the rest of phase 6:** each other app's own screens drawn the new way. That covers STORE's counter
as a sun card, TEND's plants, CREATE's counter cards and the tally that runs off its band, TOOLS' breathing ring, and WALK's
map card. Those apps already wear phases 1–2: the ROOT preset, their colour, the lowercase band, the dock.

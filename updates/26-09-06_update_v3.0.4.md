## What was done

### The two cut-offs — both mine, from v3.0.3

3.0.3 trimmed the wordmark, the day number and STORE's counter to their cap
height with `text-box:trim-both cap alphabetic`. That is right for layout — it is
what let the bands centre on their ink — but **their children still lay out in
full line boxes**, and the day number and the counter both carry
`overflow:hidden`, which clips *both* axes.

Measured in Chrome:

```
h-daynum   box 35.1px   children 54px   over top 9.4px   over bottom 9.5px
h-cost     box 35.1px   children 54px   over top 8.9px   over bottom 10.0px
```

The clip either box actually wanted is **horizontal**: a digit slides sideways
out of the day number as the date changes, and a total too wide for its space
must not run into the wordmark. Neither needs a vertical cut. Both are
`overflow:visible` with `clip-path:inset(-100% 0)` now — unbounded top and
bottom, cut at the box edges left and right.

§6 carries the rule, because anything that trims a box it also clips will hit
exactly this.

### The feature — what was finished, and when

**A completed task leaves a mark on the calendar at the minute it was ticked**: a
green dot on the rail, the time, and the name.

**Why it is not a row.** Everything else on DAY is the day as it was *planned*; a
mark is the day as it *happened*. §6 has said since 2.23 that a drawing which
quietly claims to be the plan is the one thing this app must not be — so marks
are stored **beside** `days` rather than inside one, and drawn **over** the rows
rather than among them. A completion is a fact about the afternoon, not a claim
about what was sent.

**Who can leave one.** Anything that finishes a task:

- DAY's own rows — ticking one stamps the clock time
- DO's block tiles
- DO's today list

DO's two calls sit next to the `LOG.setBlock` and `TEND.setDone` calls already
there, are optimistic like the tick itself, and are taken back if Todoist
refuses. Unticking removes the mark — the *newest* one of that name, not every
one, because the same routine finished twice in a day is two things that
happened.

**Only today can take one**, because a completion has a clock time by virtue of
happening now; ticking a row on another day marks nothing. A mark on a day that
was never exported is kept but has nothing to be drawn on until one is — it is
not thrown away for it. Marks are swept on the same keep window as the days.

**Placement** reuses the day's own stacking arithmetic: `nowOffset` was
generalised to `offsetAt(evs, per, minutes)`, so a mark lands at its minute by
walking the rows exactly the way the now line does — which matters because a row
with a floor height is taller than its duration and everything below it moves
down with it.

**Two things the first version got wrong, both caught by looking at it:**

1. Marks drawn one-per-completion landed on the same pixel when two things were
   ticked in the same minute, and printed over each other — "groceries" on top of
   "…intro". They are grouped by minute now, names joined.
2. The mark sat exactly on the now line's badge, since today is the only day
   either of them appears on. It is inset past the time column (ends ≈53px) and
   the badge (ends ≈36px) on the left, and past the row's delete and tick on the
   right — from measurements, not guesses.
